ALUMNI SPOTLIGHT

From cohort member
to community builder.

I joined The Hive Network Africa, University of Benin cohort in 2021, an experience that played a significant role in shaping my personal growth and development. Through active participation in the network's activities, I developed valuable skills in leadership, teamwork, public speaking, and collaboration, while cultivating a strong sense of responsibility, accountability, and community.

Read More...

In 2025, I graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from the University of Benin. My journey into technology was driven by passion and curiosity, particularly my fascination with computers and how they work. This interest shaped my academic and career choices, eventually leading me into software development, where I specialise in mobile application development.

Over the years, my interest in technology has evolved beyond writing code to understanding problems, exploring opportunities, and building solutions that create real value. I am passionate about leveraging technology to transform business ideas into sustainable, scalable, and world-class digital products. I believe in continuous learning, innovation, and constantly challenging myself to grow both professionally and personally.

Beyond software development, I have a strong interest in entrepreneurship and the possibilities that technology creates for businesses and society. I aspire to build innovative products and ventures that solve meaningful problems, contribute to the growth of the digital ecosystem, and leave a lasting impact.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Who qualifies to join The Hive Alumni Network?

Any individual who has successfully completed a Hive Network cohort program, campus training tour, or leadership fellowship across accredited institutions is automatically eligible to join.

How can I update my profile details on the directory?

Log in to your member portal or contact the administrative team to request updates to your current role, organization, profile tags, and contact links.

How do I launch a regional chapter in my city or state?

Reach out directly to the Alumni Relations Lead via email. We provide chapter guidelines, funding support, and logistical frameworks to establish recognized regional hubs.
