OUR TIMELINE

The Journey So Far

From a singular campus hub to a national student-led innovation network.

2015

The Genesis at UNIBEN

Launched as a pilot community project at the University of Benin with 40 visionary students, focusing on technical skill-sharing and peer-led ecosystem building.

2024

Regional Expansion

Expanded into UNILAG, OAU, and UI, standardizing our executive operational frameworks and establishing our corporate internship program.

2025

Pan-Nigerian Footprint

Welcomed UNN, ABU, and FUTA into the network, deploying our signature decentralized campus build-athons and securing cross-border venture capital partnerships.

WHAT WE DO

Ongoing Cohort Frameworks

Standardized learning tracks actively running across all university chapters simultaneously.

💡

The Campus Training Tour

Our flagship annual national conference. To provide a high-intensity, immersive experience in leadership, entrepreneurship, and personal development for university students and young professionals.

🚀

The Creative Youth Mentoring Program

To provide foundational leadership, values, and career guidance to senior secondary school students, shaping their character and ambitions before university.

🤝

The Creative Youth Arts & Science Fairs

A biennial platform to identify, showcase, and fund the most innovative entrepreneurial ideas from young creatives and scientists.

🏆

The African Achiever & African Business Summit

To celebrate role models and facilitate high-level strategic knowledge exchange.

STRUCTURE

How Campus Cohorts Function

Every university cohort operates under a standardized governance and delivery model managed by trained student executive leads.

01

Executive Leadership

Each campus cohort is governed by an elected Student Executive Board guided by a national director.

02

Bi-Weekly Hub Meets

Regular physical and virtual learning sessions, peer reviews, and skill-building modules on campus.

03

Campus Interventions

Campus tours and leadership projects executed directly by cohort members.

04

National Network Link

Direct integration into nationwide funding and cross-university events.

How to Join an Active Cohort

Applications for our general membership tracks open once at the beginning of each academic semester.

1. Verify Status

Ensure you are a registered student at one of the universities where our cohorts are located.

2. Submit Form

Click the track link below during active recruitment cycles and fill out the details in the form.

3. Evaluation Interview

Shortlisted applicants participate in a leadership assessment session and orientation before formally becoming a full-fledged Hiver.
